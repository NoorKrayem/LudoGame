🧩 Ludo game 

👑Rules:

🪆 Number of Players:

The game can be played by 2 to 4 players. We will explain it here for two players only.

🎲 Objective of the Game:

The goal is to move all of a player’s four pieces from their designated starting area (the base) to the finish area (the central area of the board) before the other players.

🎮 How to Play:

🔸To start, the player must roll a six to move one piece from the base to the starting position. Once this piece is placed on the board, the player cannot make any other moves until at least one piece is in play.

🔸If the player already has one or more pieces in play, they can move any of them according to the number rolled on the dice.

🔸Each time a player rolls the dice and gets a number, they move a piece that many spaces along the path according to the number rolled.

🔸If a player rolls a six, they get an extra turn after making their move. This can happen up to three consecutive times.

🔸There are different types of spaces on the board. The safe spaces are those where no opponent’s piece can capture the piece standing on them.

🔸No player can place more than one piece in the same safe space.

🔸A piece cannot move to a space already occupied by one of its own pieces or by an opponent’s piece, but if the space is occupied by an opponent’s piece, the opponent’s piece is sent back to its base, and the capturing player gets another turn.

🔸If two or more pieces are on the same space, they form a barrier that cannot be passed by any opponent.

🔸If a player’s piece is sent back to the base, that piece must start again by rolling a six to leave the base.

🔸A player must roll a six to move a piece from the base to the starting position, even if there are other pieces already in play forming a barrier.

🔸Important Rule: A piece can only enter the final colored row belonging to its player.

🔸When rolling a six, the player gets an extra turn.

🔸When capturing an opponent’s piece, the player also gets an extra turn.

🚩 End of the Game:

The player who successfully moves all their pieces to the finish area first is the winner.
